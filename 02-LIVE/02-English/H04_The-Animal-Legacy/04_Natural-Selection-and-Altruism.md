
# Natural Selection as Judge

We usually consider evolution the source of our defects: the Legacy Kernel that perpetuates tribalism, short-term thinking, and status-seeking. But natural selection has a second role, one that is rarely seen. It is also the judge. Not a judge on a throne, not a judge who speaks through revelation or holy scripture — but a judge who renders verdict through outcome: what survives, what thrives, what endures. No life strategy, and no worldview, can escape this verdict.

### God as Empirical Question

Every conviction — including the religious one — is fundamentally a strategy tested by reality. Groups of people whose faith leads them to cooperation, self-restraint, and concern for tomorrow build something that makes them successful; their worldview survives with them. Groups whose picture of reality disconnects them from each other and from the future lose their footing and disappear. Thus natural selection decides, generation after generation, which conceptions of meaning and of God endure. The existence of God thereby becomes not a matter of dogma or proof, but an empirical question — and humanity answers it anew with each generation, through the way it organizes itself and survives.

### The Winning Strategy

And the direction of the verdict is taking shape. Altruism and harmonious cooperation are not noble weaknesses, but the winning survival strategy. Parasitic, antisocial, and egocentric behavior achieves quick local gains, but loses on every long term: it undermines the trust on which every complex system rests, from the small community to civilization itself. People who are altruistic and build toward the future also learn ever better how to compensate for and prevent the damage caused by those who are not. Moreover, the end accelerates itself: in a society where everyone is naturally altruistic, the friction of mutual surveillance, distrust, and abuse disappears — a multiplier effect that further amplifies the success of the entire group. Selection converges on cooperation. And cooperation converges on the state in which suffering diminishes ever further: the Free-ality.

### From Conviction to Evidence

Here our task turns. Those who wish to improve antisocial people usually reach for conviction — rational, emotional, moral. But conviction is the weakest instrument in the box: it asks for insight from those who have precisely no interest in it. The real task is twofold. First, to shape the world so that antisocial behavior can evidently no longer function as a survival strategy — not forbidden, but rendered useless, like an organism that can no longer find a host. Second, to ensure that every human being who is born carries what selection rewards: the inclination to help others and the ability to do so. Education is the great lever here — not as coercion against nature, but as its development, so that altruism in the human being can come into its full right.

Thus the animal legacy turns to good. What began as blind selection gains direction in our consciousness: the generation that understands how the judge renders its verdict can know its sentence in advance — and act accordingly. That is the Mission.
